The pinned scan is [Albert Herrmann and Georg Westermann, *Historical and Commercial Atlas of China* (1935), map 38](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Map_of_Ming_Chinese_empire_1415.jpg). The Commons page asserts US public-domain non-renewal and life-plus-70 status, while warning that other jurisdictions can differ. The atlas extracted and registered the yellow mainland and Hainan regions. See `docs/CHINA_MAP_GAPS_2026_10_28.md` and `dist/data/china-open-ming-1415.LICENSE.md` for the precise status, limitations, checksum and adaptations.
