# Borno source maps

The retained 1890, 1893 and 1898 PNGs are by historian Vincent Hiribarren,
[Borno: Territorial Evolution](https://vincenthiribarren.com/borno/maps/index.html),
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). The author describes
them as approximate reconstructions of European views, not geographically exact
borders. Source hashes and the reproducible raster extraction, graticule fit,
selection of the dark core, attribution and limitations are documented in
`world/docs/LATER_MAP_GAPS_REVIEW.md` and `world/scripts/build-later-map-gaps.mjs`.
