## china-event-xihan-007 — hold-chronology

**Dong Zhongshu recommends imperial support for Confucian learning** / 董仲舒建议朝廷兴学养士

Dong Zhongshu urged Emperor Wu to establish an academy and appoint teachers to train scholars. His advice belongs to a wider development of state-sponsored learning; it should not be compressed into a single decree that founded every institution in 134 BCE.

董仲舒向汉武帝建议兴太学、置明师，以培养士人。这些建议属于朝廷逐步发展教育制度的过程，不能压缩为前134年一道诏令即完成全部制度建设。

134 BCE is the inherited anchor; the dating of the recommendations and academy’s establishment needs separate review. / 前134年为原资料锚点；对策与太学创设的年代须分别审查。

Decision: Hold chronology: recent scholarship explicitly disputes the conventional 124 BCE foundation; do not replace one conflation with an unqualified new date.
- [Hanshu 56: Dong Zhongshu](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/漢書/卷056): Supports recommendation, not a same-year foundation.
- [Xu Jianwei: The process and date of establishing the Western Han Imperial Academy](https://ccj.pku.edu.cn/article/info?aid=787462097559621): Abstract disputes the traditional 124 BCE chronology and proposes 134–131 BCE establishment with institutional completion in 130 BCE. Do not silently adjudicate this scholarly disagreement.

## china-event-zhanguo-015 — ready

**Qin captures Chu’s capital Ying** / 秦军攻陷楚都郢

In 278 BCE, Bai Qi’s Qin army captured Ying, and the Chu court moved east. The Shiji separately tells of Qu Yuan’s death in the Miluo River. That account does not date his death to the day or year of Ying’s capture.

前278年，白起率秦军攻陷郢都，楚廷东迁。《史记》另记屈原自沉汨罗江，但该篇并未把他的死亡明确系于郢都失陷之日或之年。

278 BCE dates the capture of Ying, not the end of Chu or a securely dated death of Qu Yuan. / 前278年指郢都失陷，并非楚国灭亡，也非已确证的屈原卒年。

Decision: Separate Qi’s recovery and Qu Yuan’s death from the dated conquest; no new death date asserted.
- [Shiji 73: Bai Qi](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/史記/卷073): Supports capture of Ying, not destruction of the state of Chu.
- [Shiji 84: Qu Yuan](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/史記/卷084): Records Qu Yuan’s death at Miluo but does not assign the passage an exact year.
- [Qu Yuan and his works](https://www.xinhuanet.com/politics/2015-06/20/c_127934690.htm): Search excerpt corroborates 278 BCE capture; literary reconstruction surrounding it is not adopted.

## china-event-nansong-005 — ready

**Yue Fei’s army wins at Yancheng and Yingchang** / 岳飞军在郾城与颍昌获胜

In the 1140 campaign against Jin, Yue Fei’s army fought at Yancheng and Yingchang. The Songshi records victories at both places, with Yue Yun reinforcing Wang Gui at Yingchang. It also preserves a later account of an advance to Zhuxianzhen.

1140年对金作战中，岳飞军在郾城与颍昌交战。《宋史》记两地获胜，岳云赴颍昌增援王贵；书中还保存了进至朱仙镇的后世记述。

1140 is Shaoxing 10. The point represents the Yancheng area, not Zhuxianzhen. / 1140年为绍兴十年；地图点代表郾城一带，不是朱仙镇。

Decision: Keep the reported campaigns, remove the unqualified Zhuxianzhen victory; source criticism of that episode remains a follow-up.
- [Songshi 365: Yue Fei](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/宋史/卷365): Records Yancheng and Yingchang victories, and also a Zhuxianzhen account. Attribution is necessary: reading this text is not independent corroboration of all its episodes.

## china-landmark-068 — ready

**Emperor Taizu dies and Taizong succeeds him** / 宋太祖去世，宋太宗即位

Emperor Taizu died at Wansui Hall in 976 and his younger brother succeeded him as Taizong. The candlelight-and-axe story comes from a later account by the monk Wenying. Historian Li Tao recorded it while questioning several details.

976年，宋太祖在万岁殿去世，其弟继位为宋太宗。“烛影斧声”故事出自僧人文莹的后世记述；史家李焘收录时，对其中若干细节提出疑问。

976 dates the death and succession; the cause of death is not resolved here. / 976年为去世与继位之年；本条不判定死因。

Decision: Replace sensational mystery headline with death and succession; preserve anecdote only as attributed later writing.
- [Songshi 3: Taizu](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/宋史/卷003): Death in Wansui Hall in Kaibao 9, not an account of murder.
- [Songshi 4: Taizong](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/宋史/卷004): Search excerpt supplies succession in 976.
- [Xu Zizhi Tongjian Changbian 17](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh/續資治通鑑長編/卷017): Li Tao identifies Wenying as the source and questions details; proves the narrative’s textual status, not the death’s cause.

## china-landmark-443 — ready-with-explicit-date-decision

**Tao Yuanming leaves office at Pengze** / 陶渊明辞去彭泽令

In 405, Tao Yuanming resigned after a short term as magistrate of Pengze and returned home. His own account and later biographies explain the decision differently. His withdrawal became central to the later image of the poet living close to the land.

405年，陶渊明短暂担任彭泽令后辞官归乡。他本人的叙述与后世传记，对辞官缘由有不同解释；归隐田园成为后世陶渊明形象的重要内容。

405 dates the departure from office, not the composition of Drinking V. / 405年指辞官归乡，不是《饮酒》其五的作年。

Decision: Explicitly change 393 to 405 and refocus on departure; do not merely redate the original chrysanthemum-poem claim.
- [Tao Yuanming’s resignation from Pengze](https://www.kci.go.kr/kciportal/landing/article.kci?arti_id=ART002851551): Abstract dates office and resignation in 405 and distinguishes the poet’s explanation from later interpretations. Does not date Drinking V.

## china-landmark-039 — ready

**Sun Quan moves his capital from Wuchang to Jianye** / 孙权由武昌迁都建业

Sun Quan became emperor at Wuchang in the fourth lunar month of 229. In the ninth month he moved the capital to Jianye, present-day Nanjing, leaving his heir Sun Deng at Wuchang with Lu Xun’s assistance.

229年农历四月，孙权在武昌称帝；九月迁都建业，即今南京，留太子孙登镇守武昌，由陆逊辅佐。

Both events fall in 229; the map marks Jianye, the later capital, not the accession site. / 两事均在229年；地图标示迁入的建业，不是称帝地点。

Decision: Correct the sequence and distinguish historical Wuchang (Ezhou) from modern Wuhan’s Wuchang district.
- [Ezhou historical chronology](https://www.ezhou.gov.cn/zjez/ezgk/lsyg/201902/t20190222_176954.html): April 229 accession at Wuchang precedes September move to Jianye; months follow the historical calendar.

## china-landmark-225 — ready

**Shao prefecture becomes Baoqing prefecture** / 邵州升为宝庆府

Emperor Lizong succeeded to the Song throne in 1224. His Baoqing era began in 1225, when Shao prefecture was elevated to Baoqing prefecture, at present-day Shaoyang. Lizong had previously held the title of Shaozhou defence commissioner.

宋理宗于1224年即位，1225年改元宝庆，并将邵州升为宝庆府，即今邵阳。理宗此前曾获授邵州防御使。

1225 dates the era change and prefecture elevation; the accession was in 1224. / 1225年指改元与升府；即位在1224年。

Decision: Remove conflated accession year and unsupported residence implication.
- [Songshi 41: Lizong](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/宋史/卷041): Accession in Jiading 17 (1224); Baoqing era begins following year.
- [Baoqing old city](https://wwj.hunan.gov.cn/wwj/c100310/c100314/201411/t20141119_10483043.html): Supports prefecture elevation in 1225.

## china-event-ming-019 — ready

**Wang Zhi and Zhu Yong campaign against Jianzhou Jurchens** / 汪直与朱永出兵建州女真

In 1479, Wang Zhi inspected the northeastern frontier and supervised a campaign commanded by Zhu Yong against Jianzhou Jurchens. Wang’s earlier rise had been tied to the Western Depot, established under his leadership in 1477.

1479年，汪直巡察东北边疆，并监军参与朱永统率的对建州女真用兵。汪直此前因西厂而得势；西厂于1477年设立，由他掌领。

1479 anchors the frontier campaign; the Western Depot was established two years earlier. / 1479年为边疆军事行动的锚点；西厂设于两年前。

Decision: Narrow to Mingshi biography: Joseon coordination and the exact battlefield are not established by this source.
- [Mingshi 304: Wang Zhi](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/明史/卷304): Western Depot follows Chenghua 12 (1476), hence 1477. Chenghua 15 (1479) frontier tour leads into campaign with Zhu Yong; no independent verification here of Joseon coordination or precise battlefield.

## china-event-ming-013 — ready

**Ming forces withdraw after the Lam Son victories** / 蓝山军获胜后，明军撤退

After Lam Son victories in 1427, Ming commanders agreed to withdraw from Vietnam. Le Loi became emperor in 1428, founding the Later Le dynasty. Withdrawal and dynastic foundation belong to successive years.

1427年蓝山军获胜后，明军将领议和撤出越南。黎利于1428年称帝，开创后黎朝；撤军与建朝分属前后两年。

1427 anchors the agreement and withdrawal sequence; 1428 dates Le Loi’s accession. / 1427年为议和撤军过程的时间锚点；黎利称帝在1428年。

Decision: Separate withdrawal from foundation; no claim that all troops crossed the border on one converted Gregorian day.
- [Xuong Giang citadel](https://baotanglichsu.vn/vi/Articles/1508/51632/phe-thanh-xuong-giang.html): Supports withdrawal after negotiations in 1427; calendar conversion and completion date are not adjudicated.
- [Le Loi and the founding of the Le dynasty](https://baotanglichsu.vn/vi/Articles/2001/66142/loi-sam-truyen-ve-le-loi.html): Supports accession in 1428; surrounding prophetic anecdotes not adopted.
