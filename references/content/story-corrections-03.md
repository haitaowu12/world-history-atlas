**Xie An accepts office under Huan Wen** / 谢安接受桓温征召出仕

After repeatedly declining appointments while living in Kuaiji, Xie An joined Huan Wen’s staff in 360. The Jinshu says he was already over forty when he decided to pursue office after his brother Xie Wan’s dismissal.

谢安寓居会稽期间多次辞谢征辟，360年接受桓温征召入幕。《晋书》记载，弟弟谢万被黜后，他才有意出仕，当时已四十余岁。

Date: 360 is Shengping 4. Forty-plus is his age, not the duration of a retreat.

时间：360年为升平四年；四十余指年龄，并非隐居时长。

Decision: Retain focal-event year and representative coordinates.

- [Jinshu 79 — Xie An](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/晉書/卷079): Forty-plus describes age; the following paragraph records service as Huan Wen’s sima.

- [Tongjian zonglei 15, second half — Xie An](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hant/通鑑總類_(四庫全書本)/卷15下): Places the episode in Shengping 4 (360); corroborates age over forty and Huan Wen appointment.

Carving at Longmen began around 493 as Luoyang became the Northern Wei capital. Imperial and other patrons developed a major Buddhist cave complex. The Central Binyang Cave belongs to a later memorial programme under Emperor Xuanwu; its relief of Xiaowen dates to about 522–523.

约493年，洛阳成为北魏都城之际，龙门开始开窟。皇室及其他施主共同营造了佛教石窟群。宾阳中洞属于宣武帝时期的后续纪念工程，其中孝文帝礼佛浮雕约作于522至523年。

Date: About 493 for the beginning of carving; the featured Binyang relief is about three decades later.

时间：始凿约在493年；所示宾阳浮雕晚约三十年。

Decision: Approve 495→493 for start/end with approximate dateNote. Existing 112.47,34.55 retained only as regional point; UNESCO reference is 112.4667,34.4667 (not proposed as an exact cave coordinate).

- [Longmen Grottoes — ICOMOS evaluation 2000](https://whc.unesco.org/document/1076): Supports initial carving chronology 493 and the later Xuanwu/Binyang memorial programme.

- [Longmen Grottoes, Luoyang](https://english.henan.gov.cn/2020/06-06/1545766.html): Supports approximate rather than exact-day dating of the beginning.

- [Emperor Xiaowen and entourage (35.146)](https://www.metmuseum.org/art/collection/search/42707): Relief is a posthumous memorial to Xiaowen commissioned under Xuanwu, not the founding campaign.

### china-landmark-526 — hold-chronology

**Night Revels of Han Xizai: a Southern Tang story in a later copy** / 《韩熙载夜宴图》：宋摹本中的南唐故事

The Palace Museum’s Night Revels of Han Xizai preserves a composition attributed to Gu Hongzhong in a Song-dynasty copy. Later texts give different explanations for the commission, including Li Yu sending the painter to observe Han’s gathering. The surviving scroll is not a directly authenticated record of such surveillance.

故宫博物院藏《韩熙载夜宴图》为宋摹本，构图归于顾闳中。后世文献对创作缘由有不同解释，包括李煜派画家窥察韩熙载宴饮的说法。传世画卷不能直接证实这次窥察。

Date: The story is provisionally placed around 961–967; the surviving copy is Song. The inherited exact 960 date is unsuitable.

时间：故事暂约系于961至967年；传世摹本属宋代。原资料精确的960年不宜沿用。

Decision: 961–967 is supported only by the museum publication search excerpt, not extracted full-page text. Keep held; verify the full discussion before replacing 960. No China06 portrait should substitute for the actual painting.

- [Night Revels of Han Xizai (新00147326)](https://www.dpm.org.cn/collection/paint/228200.html): Identifies surviving work as Song copy and distinguishes two transmitted commission explanations.

- [The Story of Night Revels of Han Xizai](https://www.dpm.org.cn/journal_detail/261914.html): Provisional approximate story interval 961–967; full page extraction did not expose this text. Do not treat as verified exact commission date.
