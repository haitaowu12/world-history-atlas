## china-landmark-378 — ready

**Liu Yuxi is demoted to Langzhou / 刘禹锡被贬为朗州司马**

After the reform movement failed in 805, Liu Yuxi was demoted to Langzhou, present-day Changde. He spent the following years there before returning to the capital in 815. His Bamboo Branch Lyrics belong to a different setting: their preface describes hearing local singers at Jianping.

805年革新失败后，刘禹锡被贬为朗州司马，朗州即今常德。他在当地度过此后数年，815年奉召回京。《竹枝词》属于另一创作场景，其序写的是在建平听当地人联歌。

805 anchors the demotion, not the composition of Bamboo Branch Lyrics. / 805年指贬任朗州，不是《竹枝词》的创作年。

Decision: Separate the Langzhou exile from the Jianping song preface.

- [Liu Yuxi: public service and literary tradition](https://www.gdjct.gd.gov.cn/zhyw/content/post_62863.html): 824 Hezhou appointment is stated separately from room anecdote introduced as tradition. **Liu Yuxi becomes prefect of Hezhou / 刘禹锡出任和州刺史**

In 824, Liu Yuxi moved from Kuizhou to serve as prefect of Hezhou, in present-day Anhui. Local tradition connects his modest dwelling there with Inscription on My Humble Room, a text celebrating virtue over material luxury. Its authorship and place of composition have been debated.

824年，刘禹锡由夔州转任和州刺史，和州位于今安徽。地方传说把他在此的简朴居所与《陋室铭》相联系，文章重德行而轻物质奢华；其作者归属和写作地点仍有学术争论。

824 dates the appointment; it is not an established composition date for the inscription. / 824年指任职，不是已确证的《陋室铭》作年。

Decision: Anchor the appointment and distinguish literary tradition from established chronology.

## china-landmark-383 — ready

**Fan Zhongyan takes charge of Yan prefecture’s frontier defence / 范仲淹主持延州边防**

In 1040, Fan Zhongyan took charge of Yan prefecture during the war with Western Xia. The Songshi describes him reorganizing its troops and strengthening frontier positions. Dashun fort belonged to his later Qingzhou command: the Song Huiyao dates its construction to 1042.

1040年，对西夏战争期间，范仲淹兼知延州。《宋史》记他整编州兵、加强边防；大顺城则属于此后庆州任上的建设，《宋会要辑稿》系其筑城于1042年。

1040 anchors the Yan posting, not Dashun’s construction or a dated autumn lyric. / 1040年指延州任职，不是大顺城筑城年，也不用于确定秋词作年。

Decision: Separate places and years; remove the unverified poem-composition claim.

- [Songshi 314: Fan Zhongyan](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/宋史/卷314): Yan posting, reorganization and subsequent Qingzhou/Dashun sequence, not poem date. Read: Direct web connector page passage; web-5.json, lines 146–150. Passage: 延州諸砦多失守，仲淹自請行，遷戶部郎中兼知延州。

- [Su Shunqin and Ouyang Xiu](https://dfzb.suzhou.gov.cn/dfzb/fzxh/200902/4718f0adbda74b12afc80eab95a1fda6.shtml): Dates frontier appointment sequence to 1040. Read: Direct web connector page passage; web-6.json, line 24. Passage: 范仲淹于1040年复“天章阁待制，知永兴军”，不久，为陕西都转运使，兼知延州。

## china-landmark-480 — ready

**Yu Qian begins his Henan and Shanxi administration / 于谦开始巡抚河南、山西**

Yu Qian was appointed to oversee Henan and Shanxi in 1430. The Mingshi describes grain relief, support for displaced households and visits to the districts under his charge. It counts nineteen years in office, including interruptions for mourning, before his recall to Beijing in 1448.

1430年，于谦受命巡抚河南、山西。《明史》记他发粮赈济、安置流民，并巡视所部；书中称其前后在任十九年，其间曾归乡治丧，1448年奉召入京。

1430 dates the appointment. Nineteen years is the Mingshi’s inclusive account of the tenure, not nineteen uninterrupted elapsed years. / 1430年指任命；十九年是《明史》对前后任期的计数，不是连续任职满十九周年。

Decision: Retain the sourced tenure as an attributed span; do not date the Two Sleeves Clear poem to 1430. The injustice cases in the biography precede this tenure in Jiangxi.

## china-landmark-481 — ready

**Yu Qian helps defend Beijing against Esen / 于谦主持北京防御，抵御也先**

In 1449, Yu Qian organized Beijing’s defence against Esen’s Oirat army. The Mingshi reports 220,000 troops deployed outside the city’s nine gates, with Yu Qian and Shi Heng commanding at Desheng Gate. Its account describes fighting at several gates before Esen withdrew.

1449年，于谦组织北京防御，抵御也先率领的瓦剌军。《明史》记二十二万军分列九门之外，于谦与石亨在德胜门督战；战事涉及多处城门，随后也先撤军。

1449 dates the defence. The reported 220,000 is a citywide deployment, not the force at Desheng Gate alone. / 1449年指北京保卫战；史书记载的二十二万是九门部署总数，不是德胜门一处兵力。

Decision: Attribute the troop figure and correct its geographic scope.

## china-landmark-446 — hold-exact-chronology

**Lu You and the Shen Garden reunion tradition / 陆游与沈园重逢传说**

Zhou Mi’s later Qidong Yeyu tells of Lu You meeting his former wife, Lady Tang, at Shen Garden and writing Phoenix Hairpin on its wall. It places the encounter in 1155. The story became central to the garden’s literary associations, but the exact date and details remain matters of textual interpretation.

周密后来的《齐东野语》记陆游在沈园遇见前妻唐氏，并在园壁题《钗头凤》，将此事系于1155年。这一故事成为沈园文学记忆的重要内容，但具体日期与细节仍需辨析不同文献。

1155 is the date assigned by Zhou Mi’s anecdote, not an independently established encounter date. / 1155年是周密笔记对故事的系年，不是独立确证的相逢日期。

Decision: Keep held unless the owner explicitly accepts a dated literary-tradition record; do not present as a verified encounter or silently replace the year.

- [Qidong Yeyu 1: Lu You remembers his former wife](https://zh.wikisource.org/zh-hans/齊東野語/卷一): Later anecdote explicitly dates scene to Shaoxing yihai (1155); passage calls wife Tang shi, not given name Wan. Same section quotes a later poem recalling forty years, so exact chronology is not independently resolved. Read: Direct web connector page passage; web-4.json, lines 273–280. Passage: 尝以春日出游，相遇于禹迹寺南之沈氏园。 / 为赋《钗头凤》一词，题园壁间 / 实绍兴乙亥岁也。
