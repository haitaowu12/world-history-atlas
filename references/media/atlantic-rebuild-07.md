## Curatorial limits

- Benin plaque: cast brass, not a generic bronze; specific Portuguese/manilla imagery addresses the entry’s diplomatic exchange. The 1540–1570 object predates 1897. No unsupported item-specific seizure history is asserted.
- Moche bottle: 500–800 CE, within the entry’s broader first–eighth-century range; the portrait does not identify a named ruler.
- Olmec mask: serpentinite and albitite, not assumed jade. No precise archaeological findspot or wearing practice inferred.
- Chola Nataraja: late twelfth–early thirteenth century; public processional use is a type-level curatorial account, not a known royal commission or one named temple.
- Gandharan Maitreya: about third century, gray schist, religious figure and donors; supports Kushan-era workshop networks without implying a royal commission or fixed political borders.
- Nasca pottery was considered at discovery only but not acquired: the available entry fit was less direct than these five. Existing Inka tunic was not reused.
