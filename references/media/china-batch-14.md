## Shi Kefa remembered after the fall of Yangzhou
扬州城破之后的史可法纪念像

Portrait of Shi Kefa by Li Shizhuo, catalogued as a Qing-dynasty work. The painter signs it as a respectful copy. Its date is not specified by the museum; 1645 dates the Yangzhou defense and Shi’s death, not this painting. The scroll also carries later inscriptions, including one dated 1779.
李世倬所绘史可法像，馆方归于清代，作者署“敬摹”。馆方未给出画幅的确切创作年；1645年是扬州守城与史氏去世的年份，并非此画年代。同卷还有后世题跋，包括1779年的一则。

This later likeness commemorates the named defender of Yangzhou in 1645. The same scroll includes a family letter describing the siege, but the selected image is the portrait section. Neither the portrait nor its laudatory title is an eyewitness record of the fighting.
这幅后世肖像纪念1645年扬州守将史可法。同卷还收有描述围城的家书，但所选图像是肖像部分。画面及其颂扬性题名均非战斗现场实录。
