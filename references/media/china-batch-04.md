## Dating and editorial choices

Collection-level QA supersedes the initial Commons-only dating. Sima Guang is now historical-artwork: the NPM says the portrait was added later to his 1086 appointment document, and its own date is unestablished. The 1019–1086 lifespan is not treated as an image date. Kublai is now anonymous historical-artwork with an unestablished exact date: the NPM original record explicitly gives anonymous authorship, and its album interpretation leaves authors and dates open. The draft no longer repeats the Commons 1294, Araniko, posthumous, or rejuvenation assertions.

All three Thirteen Emperors details now link the MFA-contributed collection record 31.643, whose dating explicitly includes later replacement. The date field retains this qualification and does not assign the whole-scroll date confidently to each panel. Liu Xiu's early-group detail explicitly carries the later-replacement caveat. Both Sui details retain their individual visual interpretations while their captions and date fields disclose the composite scroll's status. No image bytes changed.

## Rejected or deferred candidates

- Wu Zetian, File:唐后行從圖 (局部) - 武則天.png: metadata calls this Tang and “likely Zhang Xuan,” but credits secondary media pages. Do not propagate this as a verified contemporary likeness without a collection record.
- Zhang Juzheng, File:張居正-清晰版.jpeg: modern upload/Exif date 2018, “Own work,” and garbled English object description do not establish the historical artwork's date or provenance. Defer.
- Han Wudi, File:漢武帝.jpg: the 2009 date is not a historical artwork date; source calls it an ancient-book portrait without identifying the edition. Defer.
- Wang Anshi, File:Wang Anshi.jpg: explicit unknown artwork date and weakly specified former palace provenance. Defer pending an identifiable edition or collection record.
- Liu Bang, File:Liu-bang.jpg: metadata says Ming but cites a history blog and no identifiable collection; final pixels look like a strongly colored reproduction. Insufficient reproduction history for this pass. Download and receipt retained as rejected research, not included in shard.

## Entry issues noticed, not modified

- `china-event-donghan-001` English title says Liu Xiu proclaimed the dynasty “at Luoyang”; Chinese separates accession and establishing the capital. Coronation location and later capital should be checked separately. No additional event association admitted.
- `china-event-sui-001` combines founding Sui with “at Daxing”; the founding and construction of the new capital should be checked as separate dates. No additional event association admitted.
- `china-event-xihan-004` says abolition of “corporal punishment” broadly, whereas the richer person entry carefully says specified mutilating punishments. Prefer the narrower wording in a future correction; not linked in this batch.

These are review flags, not edits or newly verified historical corrections.
