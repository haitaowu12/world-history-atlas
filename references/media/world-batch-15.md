# World batch 15: a printing block for Hiroshige's fish

Reviewed 11 November 2026. One image, one new target: `edo-urban-culture`.

- Object: [F1904.100, National Museum of Asian Art](https://asia.si.edu/object/F1904.100/), a woodblock for Utagawa Hiroshige's design of olive halibut, black rockfish and cherry blossom, from the untitled series commonly called Large Fish; Japan, c. 1840–1842. Wood, 26.8 × 39.3 × 1.5 cm.
- The carved fish, flowers and lettering expose the material production behind the entry's discussion of urban woodblock prints. It is a printing tool, not a paper impression. The catalogue identifies the design artist, not the carver or printer.
- Rights: the object and selected first image FS-8002_12 both explicitly CC0 in the [official item-bearing Smithsonian bulk record](https://smithsonian-open-access.s3-us-west-2.amazonaws.com/metadata/edan/fsg/20.txt). Gift of Charles Lang Freer. The official screen JPEG is preserved unchanged.
- Full item metadata, bilingual text, target record, hash/dimensions and pixel-review evidence: `research/v18/world-batch-15/`.
- Context source already attached to the full target entry: [Art of the Edo Period (1615–1868)](https://www.metmuseum.org/essays/art-of-the-edo-period-1615-1868).
