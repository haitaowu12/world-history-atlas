## Night Revels: an old copy of the final scene
《韩熙载夜宴图》末段的旧摹本

An old copy of the final scene of Night Revels of Han Xizai. The National Palace Museum does not consider this the original by Gu Hongzhong; its precise production date is not established.
《韩熙载夜宴图》末段的旧摹本。故宫说明不认为此卷是顾闳中原作；制作的确切年代未定。

The figures, furniture and accompanying colophon show how a Southern Tang court story survived through copying and connoisseurship. The transmitted story says a ruler sent a painter to observe Han’s parties; this surviving object does not establish the exact occasion or year.
人物、家具和卷后题跋展示南唐宫廷故事如何经摹写与鉴赏传承。流传的故事称君主派画家观察韩氏夜宴，现存画卷却不能确定具体场合与年份。

## Night Revels reinvented in a Ming garden
明代庭园中重构的韩熙载夜宴

A Ming-dynasty adaptation of Han Xizai’s Night Revels, catalogued under Tang Yin. The museum’s attribution discussion identifies it as another Ming artist’s work bearing Tang Yin’s name.
韩熙载夜宴题材的明代改绘，馆藏题名归于唐寅；馆方鉴定说明认为实为其他明人附名之作。

The artist moves the drumming-and-dance episode into a garden furnished with Ming chairs, tables and a screen. Comparing it with the old scroll copy reveals how painters remade a tenth-century story for later viewers.
画家把击鼓观舞情节移入庭园，布置明式椅、桌和屏风。与旧摹卷并观，可见画家如何为后世观众重新塑造十世纪故事。
