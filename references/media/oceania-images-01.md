## Admitted evidence and rights receipts

1. Australian War Memorial P00670.001, https://www.awm.gov.au/collection/C38131 . Source HTML: awm-c38131.html. The item has a public-domain marker directly beside its photograph metadata, rather than merely licensing catalogue text. awm-copyright.html, from https://www.awm.gov.au/about/organisation/corporate/copyright , separately says public-domain images can be copied from the website and reproduced elsewhere without permission. This rule is distinct from the policy's other categories including noncommercial-licensed photographs. The exact official image URL appears in both the item download link and embedded mediaData: https://s3-ap-southeast-2.amazonaws.com/awm-media/collection/P00670.001/screen/3904203.JPG . Download HTTP 200; 238574 bytes; 640 × 499; SHA-256 7399dedf86c2028eb3d91d4daf9eb9e16f4d52166b1d5e2f06d1ceddbb38dda4.

Pixel inspection: dark monochrome historic photograph of crowded uniformed procession, French Arch with legible Federation welcome, street flags, black borders and original AWM accession/credit strip below. No local cropping or alteration. Modest 640-pixel source resolution, suitable for a contextual image rather than full-screen detail. Date given only as 1901; no unsupported exact day. The scene records public ceremony and does not represent universal political inclusion.

2. Museums Victoria item 2052835, media 675839, https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/items/2052835 . Source HTML: mv-badge.html. Its embedded mediaModel identifies the particular large JPEG, photographer Jon Augier, Museums Victoria, and CC BY Attribution 4.0 International, Open:true. This is image-specific, not catalogue metadata licensing. Exact official large image: https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/content/media/39/675839-large.jpg . Download HTTP 200; 419609 bytes; 2181 × 2171; SHA-256 e81142adfec3ce38247a0ea985b7cb666aa9764f5bbf4f11666080a85f1a05d6.

Pixel inspection: round off-white pin badge against grey background, four lines of black lettering VOTE / YES / FOR / ABORIGINES. Surface wear visible; whole circular object retained. No local alteration. Caption distinguishes modern object photography from the c.1967 object. Historical wording is contextualized, and interpretation explicitly avoids the citizenship/voting-rights misconception.

Bilingual title, alt, caption, interpretation, rights note and transformation declaration are in proposed-shard.json. Context uses the original target records' National Museum of Australia sources; English source titles are preserved in both language slots rather than inventing translated official titles.
