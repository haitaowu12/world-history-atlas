## Sources, rights and dates

Individual `*-metadata.json` and `*-alternatives.json` files preserve Commons discovery and rights metadata. `commons-source-pages.json` retains exact source revisions; `source-page-index.json` identifies each admitted revision. Institutional historical pages are saved as `*-context.html` and extracted text, with web-tool fallback receipts for Brewarrina and the Pyu cities. Photograph dates are labelled separately from bilingual `historicalPeriod` fields. Each item preserves creator, institution, image/source/license URLs and unchanged source-delivery bytes.

The photographed subjects are ancient, medieval or early-modern original fabric, historic maintained urban landscapes, or traditional landscape engineering. Modern maintenance, protective fittings and background buildings are incidental to those identified subjects. Restoration is explicit for Ahu Tongariki and Tiwanaku. There are no generated reconstructions or admitted modern commissioned sculptures. Each image’s photographic license remains independent of the age of its subject.

## Exclusions and historical cautions

Seven proposed sites were excluded rather than filled with weak substitutes: Kuk, Budj Bim, Djenné-Djeno, Lapaha, Vat Phou, Taputapuātea and Pulemelei. Taputapuātea’s modern-looking statue appears even in a wider Raiatea photo; the separate Rarotonga namesake was also excluded. Pulemelei’s delivered pixels showed vegetation, not legible monument fabric. The first Chavín photo showed the modern visitor entrance and was replaced by the actual ancient El Castillo complex. Joya de Cerén bird photographs were excluded.

Existing atlas distinctions were preserved: Ifugao’s recent archaeological chronology is not replaced by UNESCO’s older two-thousand-year claim; Site 1 jars do not inherit dates from Site 2 samples; Rapa Nui’s disputed early settlement date is not repeated; Kuélap’s tentative submission is not described as a World Heritage inscription. Taj Mahal’s source has differing 1631/1632 start statements, so image metadata uses the 1630s. No historical record text was changed. Details are in `historical-review-notes.json`.
