## Proposed connections

- **Jingyuan Mutiny and Fengtian (783):** Dezong is the left-hand ruler in the existing Xuanzong opening. The [NPM album inventory](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/1892?dep=P) puts Xuanzong and Dezong in opening 14, leaves 27 and 28. Visible inscriptions and accession strips independently confirm position. The story centers Dezong’s flight, not a passing imperial reference.
- **Xie Lingyun at Yongjia:** Xie is at left opposite Tao Yuanming in opening 11, leaves 22 and 21, of [NPM’s historical-worthies album](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/1890?dep=P). The [Songshu biography](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/宋書/卷67) directly connects his Yongjia appointment, travel and poetry. The image is a portrait of the principal poet, not a painting of his journey or a dated lifetime likeness.
- **Gaozong’s Wenzhou refuge (1130):** the [NPM portrait](https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/14767?dep=P) names Gaozong, personal name Gou, on the facing inscription. [Songshi 26](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/宋史/卷026) records the maritime arrival and Wenzhou stay; local historical reporting corroborates the Jiangxin stop.
- **Gaozong and Lin’an (1138):** the same portrait identifies the ruler central to the decision. [China Grand Canal Museum’s exhibition account](https://paper.people.com.cn/rmrb/pc/content/202512/27/content_30127489.html) distinguishes the formal residence, xingzaisuo, from the city’s function as the de facto capital. The bilingual association retains that distinction.
