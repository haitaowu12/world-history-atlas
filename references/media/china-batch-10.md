## A court account of the Torghut arrival
宫廷笔下的土尔扈特东迁
Status: ready

Qian Chenqun’s transcription opens with Qianlong’s 1771 text on Ubashi and the Torghut migration. The copying itself is not separately dated.
钱陈群书册开篇为乾隆1771年关于渥巴锡及土尔扈特东迁的文字，抄写时间未另行注明。

The opening names the migrating community and reports its arrival at Ili. Its language of submission reveals how the Qing court framed the movement, rather than giving the migrants’ own account.
开篇点明迁徙群体并记其抵达伊犁；“归顺”的措辞显示清廷如何解释此事，并非迁徙者自身的叙述。

Rights: 清錢陳群書御製頒和賜詩 冊。國立故宮博物院，台北，CC BY 4.0 @ www.npm.gov.tw
Date: Qing dynasty; transcribes an imperial text of 1771
清代；抄录1771年御制文字

## The white eagle presented by a Torghut noble
土尔扈特贵族进献的白鹰
Status: ready

Ignaz Sichelbarth painted the white eagle presented by the Torghut noble Shilakouken. Yu Minzhong’s inscription dates to 1773, the first ten days of the eighth lunar month.
艾启蒙绘土尔扈特贝子锡喇扣肯所进白鹰；于敏中题记纪年1773年农历八月上旬。

The named gift makes the court relationships following the Torghut migration tangible. The inscription falls two years after the 1771 arrival; the image concerns a later tribute encounter, not the journey itself.
具名贡物使土尔扈特迁徙之后的宫廷关系可见；题记在1771年抵达的两年之后，图画呈现后续进贡交往，而不是迁徙途中。

Source: https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/6717?dep=P
Rights: 清艾啟蒙畫土爾扈特白鷹 軸。國立故宮博物院，台北，CC BY 4.0 @ www.npm.gov.tw
Date: Qing dynasty; inscription dated 1773, eighth lunar month, first ten days
清代；题记为1773年农历八月上旬

## A Chenghua cup combining blue and coloured enamels
青花与彩料相合的成化斗彩杯
Status: ready

Doucai cup, Chenghua reign, 1465–1487. Underglaze blue outlines are combined with red, yellow and green overglaze enamels.
成化时期（1465—1487年）斗彩杯，以釉下青花勾线，配合釉上红、黄、绿彩。

The Ming narrative describes a varied material culture. This cup makes one ceramic process visible: decoration required coordinating colour applied below and above the glaze. Its scholarly imagery brings literary taste into a small vessel.
明代叙述强调多样的物质文化；此杯呈现一种具体制瓷工艺，即协调釉下与釉上的彩饰，并将文人趣味带入小型器皿。

Source: https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/2494?dep=U
Rights: 鬥彩人物圖杯。國立故宮博物院，台北，CC BY 4.0 @ www.npm.gov.tw
Date: Chenghua reign, 1465–1487
成化时期，1465—1487年

## Guo Xi’s Early Spring
郭熙《早春图》
Status: ready

Early Spring, signed and dated 1072 by Guo Xi. The poem at upper right was added by Qianlong in 1759.
郭熙款署1072年的《早春图》；右上方御题诗为乾隆1759年所加。

This court painter’s ordered yet shifting mountain world directly illustrates the Northern Song entry’s discussion of artistic ideals and state service. It is an imagined inhabitable landscape, not a topographic survey.
这位宫廷画家营造秩序与变化并存的山水，直接对应北宋条目关于艺术理想与朝廷服务的讨论；它是可居游的意境，并非地形测绘。

Source: https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/47?dep=P
Rights: 宋郭熙早春圖 軸。國立故宮博物院，台北，CC BY 4.0 @ www.npm.gov.tw
Date: 1072; imperial poem added in 1759
1072年；1759年增御题诗

## Fan Kuan’s towering mountains and minute travelers
范宽笔下的巨峰与微小行旅
Status: ready

Fan Kuan’s Travelers Among Mountains and Streams, Northern Song. The work is undated; Dong Qichang’s title above the painting is a later addition.
北宋范宽《谿山行旅图》，本画未纪年；画上董其昌题签为后加。

Unlike Guo Xi’s court career, the museum reports that Fan Kuan never held official office. His manipulation of distance and scale offers a distinct artistic ideal within the Northern Song narrative, making travelers almost disappear beneath the mountain.
馆方记范宽未入朝任职，与郭熙的宫廷生涯不同；他以远近及尺度对比令行旅几乎隐没于巨峰之下，为北宋叙述提供另一种艺术理想。

Source: https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/1195?dep=P
Rights: 宋范寬谿山行旅圖 軸。國立故宮博物院，台北，CC BY 4.0 @ www.npm.gov.tw
Date: Northern Song; exact year unrecorded
北宋；具体年份未载

## Ni Zan’s landscape for a departing friend
倪瓒为送友而作的江岸山水
Status: ready

Mountains Viewed from a Riverbank, 1363, second lunar month, day 17. The museum identifies it as a farewell gift; the recipient’s name was later altered and remains uncertain.
《江岸望山图》，1363年农历二月十七日。馆方指出此为赠行之作，但受赠人姓名后经改动，身份未定。

A personally exchanged painting gives concrete form to the Yuan entry’s account of private artistic expression. The inscription, empty pavilion and broad water make the work a social gift as well as an exercise in landscape brushwork.
私人赠答的绘画具体呈现元代条目中的个人艺术表达；题诗、空亭与大幅水面，使作品既是山水笔墨实践，也是一件交往之礼。

Source: https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/602?dep=P
Rights: 元倪瓚江岸望山圖 軸。國立故宮博物院，台北，CC BY 4.0 @ www.npm.gov.tw
Date: 1363, second lunar month, day 17; later inscriptions
1363年农历二月十七日；附后人题记

## Ma Yuan’s spring walk
马远《山径春行》
Status: ready

Southern Song painting by Ma Yuan, with a couplet by Emperor Ningzong. The museum dates Ma’s court service to 1190–1224, not this undated leaf to a particular year.
南宋马远画，附宋宁宗题句；馆方所记1190—1224年为供职时期，并非此未纪年册页的确切创作年。

The small encounter, corner-weighted composition and poem directly illustrate the Southern Song narrative’s intimate landscape and poetic court painting.
小型相遇场景、偏角构图与诗句，直接呈现南宋条目中的精微山水与诗意宫廷绘画。

## Zhu Da’s spare White Plum
朱耷疏简的《白梅》
Status: ready

White Plum from Chuanqi’s Sketches from Life, dated 1659 in the collection record. Chuanqi was a name used by Zhu Da.
传綮《写生》册之《白梅》，馆藏定年1659；传綮为朱耷所用名号。

The museum identifies Zhu Da as a Ming imperial descendant who became a monk after the dynasty fell. His spare personal brushwork makes the Qing narrative’s artistic responses to dynastic change concrete, without assigning the plum an unproven political allegory.
馆方记朱耷为明宗室后裔，易代后出家；疏简个人笔墨具体呈现清代条目中的易代艺术回应，但不据此为梅花强加未经证实的政治寓意。

Source: https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/4745?dep=P
Rights: 清傳綮寫生 冊 白梅。國立故宮博物院，台北，CC BY 4.0 @ www.npm.gov.tw
Date: 1659 (museum dating)
1659年（馆藏定年）

## A Southern Song Guan-ware washer
南宋官窑青瓷洗
Status: ready

Guan-ware washer with impressed dragon decoration, Southern Song, 1127–1279. The later wooden stand calls it Ru ware, an attribution the museum rejects.
南宋（1127—1279年）官窑青瓷印花龙纹洗；后配木座称其为汝窑，馆方不采此旧定名。

The glaze and shallow form give the Southern Song narrative’s refined ceramics a specific example. The later mistaken label also shows why an imperial collection’s attribution must be distinguished from current object research.
釉色与浅器形为南宋条目中的精致陶瓷提供实例；后世误题又说明宫廷旧定名应与现今器物研究区分。

Rights: 官窯 青瓷印花龍紋洗。國立故宮博物院，台北，CC BY 4.0 @ www.npm.gov.tw
Date: Southern Song, 1127–1279
南宋，1127—1279年

## The standardization edict on a Qin measure
秦量上的统一度量衡诏书
Status: ready

Undated paper rubbing of a Qin measure bearing the forty-character standardization edict of 221 BCE. The rubbing itself is not dated to the Qin dynasty.
未纪年纸本秦量拓片，所拓为公元前221年四十字统一度量衡诏书；拓片本身并非据此定为秦代。

The inscription explicitly orders inconsistent measures to be unified. It directly documents the Qin entry’s standardization policy, while the vessel outline shows the practical measuring object carrying that order.
铭文明确要求度量不一者统一，直接对应秦条目的标准化政策；器形拓影显示承载诏令的实际量器。

Source: https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/19492?dep=P
Rights: 秦量墨拓 軸。國立故宮博物院，台北，CC BY 4.0 @ www.npm.gov.tw
Date: Rubbing undated; reproduces an edict of 221 BCE
拓制年代未载；复制公元前221年诏书

## Wen Zhengming copies the Drunken Pavilion essay
文徵明书《醉翁亭记》
Status: ready

Wen Zhengming’s copy dated 1551, seventh lunar month, day 24, of Ouyang Xiu’s Drunken Pavilion essay. It is a later copy, not the 1046 original.
文徵明1551年农历七月二十四日书欧阳修《醉翁亭记》，为后世抄本，而非1046年原稿。

The named text is the literary work central to the Chuzhou entry. A Ming calligrapher’s careful transcription shows its continuing readership centuries after Ouyang’s local service.
此篇正是滁州条目的核心文学作品；明代书家精心抄写，展示欧阳修任职数百年后的持续阅读与传播。

Rights: 明文徵明書醉翁亭記 軸。國立故宮博物院，台北，CC BY 4.0 @ www.npm.gov.tw
Date: 1551, seventh lunar month, day 24; copies an essay of 1046
1551年农历七月二十四日；抄录1046年文章

## A court commemoration of the Altishahr campaign
宫廷纪念平定回部的碑文
Status: conditional Qing020

Guanbao’s undated transcription of Qianlong’s inscription commemorating the Altishahr campaign. The pictured opening names the commemorative text; it does not date the calligraphy to 1759.
观保未纪年书乾隆《平定回部告成太学碑文》；所示开篇点明碑文题名，并未据此将书迹定为1759年。

The text celebrates the Qing victory in the emperor’s own political language. It is evidence of official commemoration, not a neutral account of the conquered communities or an image of the fighting.
文字以皇帝的政治语言赞颂清军胜利，是官方纪功的证据，而非被征服群体的中立叙述或战斗实景。

Source: https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/1562?dep=P
Rights: 清觀保平定回部告成太學碑文 卷。國立故宮博物院，台北，CC BY 4.0 @ www.npm.gov.tw
Date: Qing dynasty; calligraphy undated
清代；书迹未纪年
