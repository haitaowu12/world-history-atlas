Research-only proposal. One image, one new unique covered record.

## Ayusi remembered after the 1755 Ili campaign

1755年伊犁战役后的阿玉锡形象

Giuseppe Castiglione painted Ayusi in 1755 after his raid on Dawachi’s camp. The Qianlong emperor’s accompanying text presents the soldier’s achievement from the Qing court’s viewpoint. This is a commemorative court portrait, not an eyewitness image of combat.

郎世宁于1755年阿玉锡袭击达瓦齐营地后奉命绘像。乾隆帝的配文从清廷角度表彰战功。这是纪功宫廷肖像，并非战场实录。

The painting links a named participant to the 1755 campaign. Isolating the rider against an empty ground turns military service into an imperial image of merit. It does not independently verify the inscription’s troop totals or turn the court’s judgment of its enemies into a neutral description.

此画将一位有名可考的参与者与1755年战役相联系。空白背景突出了骑者，把军旅经历转化为帝国表彰功勋的形象；它并不独立证实题文中的兵数，也不把宫廷对敌方的评价当作中立描述。
