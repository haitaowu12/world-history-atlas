# World batch 10: work, ceremony and historical landscapes

Nine candidate images cover seven previously unillustrated entries. The set is deliberately below the 25–40 request: broad searches produced many weak matches. No generic substitute or peripheral portrait was admitted.

Only Panama and Tonga receive two images: the former compares unequal living services, the latter distinguishes seafaring technology from chiefly ceremony. All other targets receive one.
