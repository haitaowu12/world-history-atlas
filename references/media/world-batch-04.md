## Rejections and gaps

See `research/v18/world-batch-04/rejected-candidates.json` for eight explicit decisions. Restricted-image Malagan/Kuba and the alternative Leoni Galileo are excluded. A 1181–82 Seljuq-style burner is not linked to the earlier Great Seljuq political phase. A later Newton cast was visually weak at thumbnail scale; a second Luther portrait was redundant.

Priority follow-on research: named makers such as Dasoja and Ja’far; a precisely dated Encyclopédie binding plate; securely open Cibali workplace and Bengal kantha images. No additional entries were invented by this worker.
