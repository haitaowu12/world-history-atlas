## Pixel and attribution review

- Cao Cao: inspected the tall cap, beard, and heading 魏太祖像. The heading is a posthumous title, not a contemporary identification. Wang Qi and Wang Siyi are credited as compilers, not asserted to be the engraver. Commons gives approximately 1607; the book was completed in 1607 and printed in 1609. The exact impression is not established.
- Liu Bei: inspected the central imperial figure, two attendants and the explicit 蜀主劉備 inscription. MFA-contributed catalog metadata states a seventh-century scroll with later replacement. The first six figures, including Liu Bei, are generally treated as replacements; precise replacement dating is left open. The old single “7th-century Yan Liben portrait” shortcut is not repeated. Additional discussion: https://www.ebsco.com/research-starters/biography/yan-liben . The museum’s live collection page required browser verification; the MFA-contributed AMICA record and University of Chicago catalog supplied corroborating object metadata. The image bytes came from the separately public-domain Commons reproduction, not the AMICA image service.
- Zhuge Liang: inspected standing gray-robed figure, folded cap, inscriptions and red seals. National Museum of China explicitly lists the Ming standing portrait. Modern exhibition publication date is not treated as artwork date.
- Hongwu: inspected pale dragon robe, black cap, green/red belt, full frontal pose and patterned carpet. Matched the specific NPM object 中畫000311, Seated Portrait (VII), not the different (I) accession 中畫000293. Its broad Ming date cannot establish lifetime production, so the record uses historical-artwork rather than falsely classifying it as a verified lifetime portrait.
- Zhang Zhongjing: inspected a full bordered book page with the heading 醫聖張仲景 and a bearded figure holding a book. Wellcome identifies the surviving print as Wanli-period Ming, while attribution to Gan Bozong concerns its older design tradition. The official current image is Public Domain Mark; no older third-party CC BY metadata is substituted.
