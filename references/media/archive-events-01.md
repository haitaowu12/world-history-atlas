## Rights and provenance

Both official item records returned HTTP 200 and explicitly mark their photograph “Copyright expired - public domain”. This is not an inference from age. The separate AWM copyright policy at https://www.awm.gov.au/about/organisation/corporate/copyright distinguishes image categories from the website’s CC BY-NC text licence. Its public-domain section permits copying website images and reproducing them elsewhere without permission. Purchased high-quality reproductions have separate terms; neither image here was purchased or obtained through that service. Existing policy receipt: research/v18/oceania-images-01/awm-copyright.html. This batch’s concise item receipts are rights-receipts.json, download receipts download-receipts.json; raw retrieved item HTML is retained for review, not intended for public publication. No full copyrighted catalogue descriptions should be copied into public media documentation.

Pixel review: monochrome street facade, pedestrians and bicycle, flags above, large political slogans below. Text advocates Indonesian self-determination, liberty, equality and fraternity; another visible slogan threatens resistance to domination. No graphic violence. Official accession/credit strip retained. Faces are visible at small scale. This is political street expression surrounding independence, not a photograph of the proclamation ceremony or proof of an exact exposure time. Interpretation connects public demands to the subsequent revolution and diplomacy without suggesting independence was completed in a single day.

### Holocaust

Item: https://www.awm.gov.au/collection/043302 ; accession 043302; negative; photographer unspecified. Official source: https://s3-ap-southeast-2.amazonaws.com/awm-media/collection/043302/screen/4128468.JPG . Download HTTP 200; 201616 bytes; 640 × 519; SHA-256 9d1ee43b2638ba7aabef97f688efdf758df847f5f511c512e032e941d20bfc1d. Saved as images/archive-awm-043302.jpg.

Pixel review: non-graphic group of clothed camp survivors beside a wire fence. Some striped uniforms, buildings and a large mound beyond the fence. No visible corpses, open wounds or nudity. Faces are visible and potentially recognizable, but the catalogue supplies no names; no identities or individual ethnic/religious backgrounds are inferred. Suitable for a sober, contextual gallery presentation; preserve full frame and avoid face crops, sensational wording or decorative use. The source identifies Belsen survivors in Germany in 1945 and records British liberation on 15 April. It does not give the exact exposure date, so caption does not claim it was photographed on liberation day. Interpretation is explicitly limited to this camp and survivors, does not label every person Jewish, and does not imply liberation ended suffering or summarize the whole Holocaust through one image. Parent requested this explicit sensitivity review.

## Bounded search holds and exclusions

- russian-revolutions: official AWM C964851 and C387122 show generic Russian-front troops/armoured cars around 1917, not either revolution; C304983 concerns wireless operators and a post-revolution military detachment. Rejected as weak visual matches. AWM C2096308 is a late-tsarist war-loan poster, not admitted as a direct revolution image. No denied Commons or other archive images retried.
- philippine-american-war: AWM search surfaced the Congressional Medal item C318837 plus unrelated WWII Philippines material. No actual event photograph with verified reusable bytes selected; medal alone not used to replace the history’s Filipino political viewpoint.
- salt-march: no precise AWM/Museums Victoria object or photograph with verified reusable image found in the bounded search. Generic Gandhi portrait not substituted.
- south-asia-independence-partition: Museums Victoria 1949 British coins (e.g. https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/items/68141) document removal of the imperial India title, but are downstream British material culture, not evidence of Partition or migration. 1947 Indian coin https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/items/66202 has image-specific CC BY, but date alone is not enough connection. No coin admitted.
- first-fleet-colonization: Museums Victoria searches yielded later anniversary medals, including https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/items/53079 (1938) and https://collections.museumsvictoria.com.au/items/273157 (1988 Charlotte-medal commemoration). These are later commemorations, not contemporary settlement evidence; no substitution admitted. Prior NLA and SLNSW denied targets were not retried.
- Holocaust alternatives: Museums Victoria later ceramics associated with survivor Martha Ash were not selected because the direct camp photograph is more precise. Sachsenhausen uniform item 1718481 concerns non-Jewish Polish imprisonment and would require careful distinct interpretation; not used as a generic stand-in for specifically anti-Jewish genocide. Graphic Belsen art candidate AWM C176832 is AWM-licensed rather than public domain and was not downloaded.

No denied target or alternate route to one was used. Searches may return blocked-site results incidentally; none were opened or downloaded. Two selected items and their exact image downloads succeeded, with no pending/uncertain action.
