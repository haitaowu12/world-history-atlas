## Admission method

Read the complete current assembled bilingual record, not only names, titles or relatedIds. Match a named principal actor, substantive co-participant or exact named place to the existing image. Preserve original source dating and identities. Source quotations prove depicted identity and image date; they do not independently prove every inherited narrative detail. Known disputed scene claims remain out.
