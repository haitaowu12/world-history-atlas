## Held and flagged items

Zhang Juzheng’s generic portrait remains held pending an exact catalogue plate or museum record. Modern statues are excluded where the sculpture’s own rights are unverified. The Huo Qubing photograph reverse, retouched Deng crop, unsuitable framed Sun photograph and misidentified NPM flower painting were rejected or superseded.

Image associations do not silently resolve these concerns.
