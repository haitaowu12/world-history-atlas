## Sacred treasure of the Taiping kingdom

太平天国的“圣宝”

Reverse of a Taiping Tianguo coin, National Museum of History 78-01051. The obverse names the kingdom; the reverse calls its money “sacred treasure”. The exact mint and casting year are not recorded.

国立历史博物馆78-01051号太平天国钱背面。正面列国号，背面称“圣宝”；具体铸地与铸造年份未载。

Hong Xiuquan’s kingdom joined religious authority to government. The words “sacred treasure” put that claim onto money, complementing the religious publications discussed in his biography. This is evidence of the regime’s self-presentation, not a portrait of Hong or proof that its economic programmes were fully implemented.

洪秀全的政权将宗教权威与统治相结合。“圣宝”二字把这种宣示带到钱币上，与传记所述宗教出版物相互参照；它体现政权的自我表述，并非洪秀全肖像，也不能证明各项经济方案已全面实施。

## Li Zicheng’s reign name on money

钱币上的李自成永昌年号

Yongchang tongbao, a five-cash denomination of Li Zicheng’s Shun regime, catalogued to 1644–1645. This example entered the National Museum of History in 1988.

李自成大顺政权的永昌通宝折五钱，馆藏说明列铸行期为1644—1645年；本件1988年入藏国立历史博物馆。

A new reign name made the claim to rule visible in an everyday object. The coin accompanies Li’s move from rebellion to state-building, while its five-cash denomination points to administration as well as conquest. Its findspot and individual mint are not recorded.

新年号把统治主张铸进日常物件。此钱对应李自成由反叛走向建政的过程，折五面值也提示征服之外的行政需求；本件出土地与具体铸局未载。

## Han Lin’er’s Longfeng reign on a coin

钱币上的韩林儿龙凤年号

Longfeng tongbao of Han Lin’er’s late-Yuan Song regime. The museum connects this coin type with Liu Futong’s enthronement of Han at Bozhou in 1355; this example’s exact casting year is not given.

元末韩林儿宋政权的龙凤通宝。馆藏说明将这一钱种与1355年刘福通拥韩林儿于亳州称帝相连；本件具体铸年未载。

The new regime asserted authority through its own calendar and coinage. Reading the reign name on this surviving object gives the Bozhou proclamation a material counterpart, without treating the coin as an object used at the enthronement itself.

新政权通过自立年号与铸币宣示权威。实物上的龙凤年号，为亳州称帝提供了可见的物质对应，但不能将本件认作即位仪式中使用的器物。

## The Yongli court’s name survives on money

钱币保存的永历朝廷年号

Yongli tongbao with gong on the reverse, catalogued to the Yongli coinage of 1647–1662. Zhu Youlang was enthroned at Zhaoqing in 1646 and named the following year Yongli 1.

背面有“工”字的永历通宝，馆藏说明列铸行期为1647—1662年。朱由榔1646年在肇庆即位，以次年为永历元年。

A reign name on money carried the Southern Ming court’s claim beyond a palace proclamation. This coin illustrates the political authority associated with Yongli, rather than the accession ceremony itself. Its precise mint and findspot are not established in this catalog.

钱币上的年号，把南明朝廷的政治主张带出宫廷宣告的场合。本件表现与永历相连的统治权威，而非即位仪式本身；馆藏记录未确定其具体铸局与出土地。
