## Lü Bu’s halberd shot in Ming painting
明代绘画中的吕布辕门射戟

Testing Archery, catalogued under Dai Jin, Ming dynasty. The museum identifies the subject as Lü Bu shooting the halberd at the camp gate.
《太平乐事》册之《试射》，馆藏归于明代戴进，主题明确标为吕布“辕门射戟”。

A later artist turns Lü Bu’s mediation between rival forces into a display of archery. Read beside the biography’s unstable alliances, the scene shows how martial skill became a memorable way of telling his story; it is not a contemporary likeness.
后世画家把吕布在敌对军队之间的调停，表现为射术展示；与传记中不稳定的联盟并读，可见武勇如何成为讲述其故事的鲜明方式，而非当时写生的容貌。

## A road inscription reread after the conquest of Shu
灭蜀之后的道路铭文与宋人考证

Rubbing of the Baoxie road inscription and Yan Mao’s commentary, signed in 1195. The catalog’s date field says 1194, the discovery year given in the text; the date of making this paper rubbing is not established.
褒斜阁道铭文及晏袤考释的拓片，考释署1195年；馆藏年代栏为1194年，即文中所记发现年，纸本拓制时间未能确定。

The commentary explicitly links reopening the road to Zhong Hui and Deng Ai’s 263 conquest and Liu Shan’s surrender. It lets the conquest narrative be read through damaged inscriptions and a Song scholar’s reconstruction of transport history.
考释明确将阁道重通与263年钟会、邓艾灭蜀及刘禅投降联系起来，使灭蜀叙事能通过残损铭文与宋人对交通史的重建来阅读。

Rights: 漢潘宗伯等題名造橋殘石墨拓本 軸。國立故宮博物院，台北，CC BY 4.0 @ www.npm.gov.tw
Date: Rubbing undated; commentary signed 1195, recording discovery in 1194
拓制未纪年；考释署1195年，记1194年发现

## A Qing copy of the Tang Restoration Ode
清人抄写的《大唐中兴颂》

He Shaoji copied the Tang Restoration Ode in 1859. Yuan Jie composed the original text in 761; the inscription was carved in 771. This is a Qing transcription of that account.
何绍基1859年书《大唐中兴颂》，原文由元结撰于761年，771年刻石；此册为清代抄写的文本。

The opening names An Lushan’s advance and the flight of the Tang court. Its later praise of dynastic recovery makes this a partisan Tang account, transmitted through nineteenth-century calligraphy, rather than a portrait of the rebel.
开篇点出安禄山进军与唐廷出奔；后文颂扬王朝恢复，显示它是经十九世纪书法传写的唐朝立场记述，而不是叛军领袖的肖像。

Source: https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/31239?dep=P
Rights: 清何紹基書中興頌 冊。國立故宮博物院，台北，CC BY 4.0 @ www.npm.gov.tw
Date: 1859; text composed 761, original inscription carved 771
1859年；原文撰于761年，原碑刻于771年

## Liu Yong reflects on Yu Yunwen’s Caishi victory
刘墉评虞允文采石之战

Liu Yong’s undated Qing copy of a letter by the Song official Yu Yunwen, followed by Liu’s commentary. The letter concerns illness and gifts; the following note turns to military judgment.
刘墉未纪年的清代书册，抄宋代虞允文尺牍并附评论；信中谈疾病与馈赠，后面的评语转入军事判断。

The added commentary explicitly praises the scholar-official’s courage at Caishi and then criticizes his later arrangements in Shu. It preserves a later assessment of command, allowing victory to be considered beside decisions that received a different judgment.
附评明确赞许这位文臣在采石的勇略，又批评他后来的守蜀部署；这是后世对军事指挥的评价，可将胜绩与获得不同评判的决策并观。

Date: Qing dynasty; copy undated
清代；抄本未纪年
