## Interpretive gaps to resolve separately

- china-landmark-359 makes Luoyang certain; the scholarly chronology also allows Chenliu. Context flags this rather than changing the frozen record.
- china-landmark-368 says Yan Zhenqing found the remains; the museum says he sent his elder nephew Quanming. It also has mismatched Fengxiang / Fengxiang-to-Puzhou location labels. The image links only to the named surviving draft.
- china-landmark-017 compresses deaths into “the Yan clan dies”; the manuscript and museum explain particular family losses and surviving kin. The seventeen-commandery claim was not verified by the manuscript.
- china-event-beisong-021 has a longer examination roster than supported by the Su Shi passage. The association supports Su Shi only, not independent verification of all candidates.
- china-landmark-422’s wrestlers and precise arrest setting were not established by the inspected biography. The context expressly avoids treating a later court portrait as that scene.
- A tomb layout/terracotta image would teach qin-mausoleum better than this admitted person context. Likewise, waterways/engineering views would complement Su Shi’s causeway entry. No new coverage claim should count such future assets twice.
