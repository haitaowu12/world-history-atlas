## Liu Yuxi in a Qing portrait album
清代册页中的刘禹锡

Qing-dynasty commemorative portrait of the Tang poet Liu Yuxi, with its facing biography retained.
清代绘唐代诗人刘禹锡纪念像，保留对页传记。

The named poet appears beside a written life. The portrait belongs to the later reception of his writing and career, rather than to his Tang lifetime.
具名诗人与生平文字并列，展示后世如何记忆其诗文与仕宦，不是唐代生前写照。

## Wang Yangming’s sparse river landscape
王阳明疏林江岸图

王守仁山水，馆藏定年1506；自题为丙寅正月初七。

## A congratulatory album on the Jinchuan campaign
贺平金川颂册

Opening of a Qing court congratulatory album on completing the campaigns against Greater and Lesser Jinchuan. Exact production year is unrecorded.
清代祝贺平定大小金川颂册开篇；确切制作年未载。

The heading explicitly announces completion of the two Jinchuan campaigns. Its formal praise records the court’s celebration of military success and should be read as a partisan voice.

## The ritual conch sent with Fuk’anggan to Taiwan
随福康安赴台的法螺

Qianlong-period conch. Its box label records dispatch with Fuk’anggan to Taiwan in 1787 and return to the court in 1788.
乾隆时期法螺，原匣签记1787年随福康安赴台、1788年缴回宫廷。

## Sericulture attributed to Liu Songnian

Painting cataloged as Song-dynasty Silk Cultivation by Liu Songnian. The exact year is not established.
馆藏题为宋刘松年《蚕事图》，确切年代未定。

建筑与劳动者将蚕织生产安排在家庭空间；与纺织条目并读，可看见布帛背后的劳动，但精英绘画并非劳动处境的完整调查。

## The Duke Mao bronze cauldron
毛公鼎

西周晚期铜器，馆藏定为公元前827—782年；内壁长铭涉及王命与毛公。

This is one of the inscribed ritual vessels central to the Zhou entry’s account of offices and political authority. The exterior photograph shows the vessel’s form; the inscription is transcribed on the linked museum page.

## Hongwu’s edict to Xu Da
明太祖敕徐达

Undated imperial manuscript addressed to General Xu Da, cataloged as Zhu Yuanzhang’s handwriting. The whole opening is retained.
致大将军徐达的未纪年敕书，馆藏著录为朱元璋御笔；保留完整对开。

The emperor shares sacrificial meat after praying for crops with the general named in the biography. This direct address reveals the relationship between sovereign and commander through ritual favor rather than an imagined battle.
皇帝祈谷祭祀后与传记中的将军分享祭肉；直接称名的敕书通过礼仪恩赐呈现君臣关系，而非想象战场。

## Wang Yangming’s Record of the Shrine to Xiang
王阳明《象祠记》

Museum-photographed opening section of Wang Shouren’s Record of the Shrine to Xiang. The manuscript itself is undated.
王守仁《象祠记》正文开篇的馆方分段摄影；此书迹未纪年。

The text responds to a local An chief’s rebuilding of a shrine and discusses moral transformation. It offers a Ming scholar’s voice within negotiations between hereditary local authority and imperial cultural ideals.
文字回应安氏宣慰重修祠宇，并讨论教化；在世袭地方权力与帝国文化理想的互动中，留下明代士人的声音。

牟益《捣衣图》中的裁缝

牟益1240年画卷的馆方第11段摄影，将谢惠连旧诗转为女子制衣场景。

剪刀、布料与共同工作空间呈现成衣劳动；此南宋作品以唐风人物阐释诗意，并非宋代作坊的直接写照。

## Qianlong’s Ten Complete Victories in woven silk

Official photographed section 3 of a Qing kesi textile reproducing Qianlong’s Ten Complete Victories text of 1792. Textile manufacture is not separately dated.
清代缂丝乾隆《十全记》的馆方第3段摄影；所摹文字为1792年，织造年代未另载。

## Hongwu’s instructions to the Ministry of Revenue
明太祖谕户部

Edict dated 1384, the twenty-third day of the intercalary tenth lunar month, concerning household taxation and local administrators.
1384年闰十月二十三日谕旨，涉及户口税粮与地方官员。

The founder addresses how revenue instructions reach local offices and taxpayers. This surviving administrative text adds an imperial voice to the biography’s discussion of Ming institutions.
开国皇帝讨论税粮指令如何传至地方官府与纳税户；留存政务文本为其传记中的明初制度提供御前声音。

## A Ming order on southwestern military supplies
明初西南军务谕旨

Military order dated 1388, second lunar month, day 10. It discusses southwestern local chiefs, troops and grain shortages.
1388年二月初十军务谕旨，涉及西南土官、军队与缺粮。

The text names local chiefs and explicitly discusses supplying troops. It gives the regional tusi framework a concrete administrative document showing the uneven practical demands of imperial rule.
文本点名土官并明确讨论军粮，为土司制度框架提供具体政务文书，展示帝国统治在实际资源与义务上的不均衡。

Source: https://digitalarchive.npm.gov.tw/Collection/Detail/27062?dep=P
Rights: 明太祖御筆（二） 冊 右軍都督府軍務事。國立故宮博物院，台北，CC BY 4.0 @ www.npm.gov.tw
