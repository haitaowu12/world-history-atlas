# Two original explanatory graphics

Fresh source-text review on 12 November 2026 supports two distinct teaching graphics. Their facts, sources and drawing constraints are recorded in `docs/v18-knowledge-graphics-evidence.json`; content and render logic are in `dist/knowledge-graphics.mjs`.

The genome grid distinguishes approximate sequence coverage from reference diversity. The assembly diagram separates formal eligibility from practical attendance and keeps excluded groups outside the meeting path. Neither claims to be a historical artifact, exact reconstruction or quantitative population model. Both have English and Chinese labels, native keyboard disclosures and visible source links. Historical image coverage is unchanged.
