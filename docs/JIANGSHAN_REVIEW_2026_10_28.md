# Jiangshan main review — 28 October 2026

The original [Jiangshan main at 9799f0f](https://github.com/DeanChensj/jiangshan-map/tree/9799f0f61cff80922970d2a0b304e18527db7e1e) is 22 commits ahead of the atlas's prior reviewed [10fdcae](https://github.com/DeanChensj/jiangshan-map/tree/10fdcaeeb3361525e2f34bd7ca767714341565f0). This compares the original project, not the owner's separate fork's main branch. Review used static repository comparison; upstream scripts were not run.

## Selected

- The new upstream life profiles identified **Zu Chongzhi** and **Yelü Chucai** as worthwhile additions. The atlas biographies are independently written in English and Chinese from linked, inspected university scholarship and dynastic histories. They add six selected episodes and connect to existing reading paths. Zu's death year is 500 in the *Book of Southern Qi* but 501 in the St Andrews biography; a 477–479 episode is shown at its midpoint. Yelü's 1218 pin is a departure context, not the known place of summons; his western writings are not a complete itinerary. The fiscal milestone interprets a cyclical year as 1231 in sequence. Later court histories are retrospective accounts.
- A separate review overlay corrects the inherited 462 Zu landmark: that year dates the calendar submission, not a demonstrably dated calculation of pi. The pinned original identity and wording remain accessible.
- Upstream's shareable-view improvement prompted an independent implementation for the atlas: a selected ordinary map trace is rounded to three decimal places in the link, and a chosen biography episode can be restored even when two episodes share a year. Invalid or conflicting context is ignored. Existing route, reading-path and directory links keep their own state.

## Deferred or already covered

- The new historical boundaries were not imported. The upstream repository's root GPL-3.0 license does not settle rights for CHGIS/Hartwell-derived data, which its documentation subjects to separate academic/noncommercial terms. Geometry-specific attribution, rights, validity windows, and independent historical comparison remain necessary.
- Polygon label placement is already addressed by the atlas's separate interior-anchor work. The upstream regime profiles, multi-person timeline and place-name renames require further identity matching, licensing and historical review; do not overwrite stable imported IDs. The Shen Kuo profile and other upstream biographies need additional event/date review before changing existing biographies.
- Candidate dates and locations from the donor are research leads, not independent evidence. For example, a precise date or complete personal journey cannot be inferred merely from a list of upstream map stops. No upstream implementation code, new geometry, or donor biography prose was copied in this intake.

The published V14 content files, earlier import identities and existing map layers remain pinned. This is a selective review, not a claim that every upstream change has been accepted or every historical assertion independently verified.
