# China content expansion: CH1–CH3 evidence review

Checked on 2026-10-23. This batch adds 21 original bilingual records: six full biographies and fifteen connected contexts. It is independent source research, not Gemini output. It supplements the inherited corpus; it does not certify all 975 inherited events or reconstruct historical river geometry. No new timeline bands, polity fills or territorial claims are introduced.

CH1 biographies are release 1; CH2 social/material contexts form release 2 and regional/modern contexts release 3; CH3 adds the Yuan capital-water context and links Guo’s biography and the Hani water landscape. CH3 is an evidence/context contribution, not completion of the separate rivers/roads/walls functionality or geometry audit.

## Editorial decisions and limits

- Ban Zhao: c.45 and c.48 birth conventions are disclosed; both translated primary selections are hosted by Columbia, not two independent institutions. The 106 regency association is an approximate contextual milestone, not an invented appointment day. The introduction’s misleading infant-son wording is not repeated.
- Li Qingzhao: 1084 is the birth convention; death remains in the 1150s (Columbia c.1151; indexed CBDB 1155). The display endpoint is explicitly uncertain. Her afterword is read in Chinese, with a community-transcription caveat, and is not used to establish that every lyric is autobiographical. OAPEN/De Gruyter book links failed to yield inspectable full text and are not padded into the record bibliography.
- Shen Kuo, Guo Shoujing and Xu Guangqi: MacTutor is a useful university history synthesis, not a critical edition. Its inflated evaluations, suspect imperial succession detail and civilizational superiority rhetoric are excluded. Calendar and hydraulic achievements retain collaboration and institutional context. Xu’s 1607 translation is explicitly the first six Euclid books.
- Tu Youyou: 1969–2015 is an activity/recognition window, not a lifespan. Nobel pages were indexed but blocked on full retrieval; award claims are limited accordingly. Conflicting precise early crystallization dates are omitted. No clinical instructions or current treatment claims are offered.
- Song entries: university teaching pages are grounded contextual sources, but companion pages are not independent corroboration. The rice entry adds an independent Smithsonian object catalogue for a later tilling scroll; it is not used to prove Song yields. The two city sources are companion pages. Textiles adds independent museum visual evidence, examinations an independent museum essay, and books an independent museum account. Artistic representations are not treated as census data.
- Porcelain: production and market histories complement the existing 1004/1402 Jingdezhen events. The kendi is not assumed to be European export; the account retains Asian demand. Quanzhou is a network reference, not a claim that all sixteenth-century exports left that port.
- Hani, Tulou, Tusi and Kaiping are narrow place/institution entries supported by one fully inspected UNESCO dossier each. This limitation is explicit rather than disguised by repeated institutional links. Official heritage descriptions are not independent ethnography, evidence of timeless harmony or proof of peaceful political consent. Rounded focus intervals are not invented foundation dates.
- Yuan water: the 1276–1293 focus brackets the inherited calendar/canal cluster. Only the 1292 office date is used as a precise engineering-career anchor; route alignments, construction phases, water seasonality and changing coastlines remain unverified by this batch.
- Modern family: the Chinese Posters guide and primary poster belong to one research project, not independent institutions. The entry discusses the 1950 campaign, not the 1980/2001/2021 legal texts that search results can misleadingly return. It does not reproduce unsupported registration percentages or treat propaganda as outcome measurement.
- Avoided duplicates: inherited Cai Lun 105, Bi Sheng, Shen’s 1088 publication, Huang Daopo 1295/1296, Tiangong kaiwu 1637, Dujiangyan, Guo’s 1281 calendar and 1293 canal remain separate. Root owns claim corrections, Galle/Malacca replacements and linking the inherited Li Qingzhao person profile; this batch does not change those files.
