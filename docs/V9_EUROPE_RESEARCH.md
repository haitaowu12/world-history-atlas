## Delivered scope

33 entries: 2 culture, 9 event, 1 idea, 10 person, 2 place, 9 polity.

EU1 develops Roman imperial phases, the Holy Roman Empire, Christian Kyivan Rus’, Scandinavian union, the Commonwealth and Iberian dynastic connections; archaeological trade and pilgrimage histories provide non-state context. EU2 adds composite institutions, students, refugees, coercive commerce and biographies. EU3 adds labour regulation, constitutional experiments, civilian targeting, migration, Cold War civic action and scientific exile. This is a bounded implementation across all three release themes, not exhaustive European coverage.

## Decisions from source comparison

- **Havel election date:** Prague Castle gives 29 December 1989. The library timeline places the same election under a 1990 heading. The biography uses 1989 and discloses the disagreement; other obvious timeline heading issues were not imported.
- **Gernika date:** Reina Sofía’s main research portal and Spanish contextual text identify 26 April 1937. The English contextual page has 26 January 1937, an internal error. The entry follows April and does not repeat a contested casualty total.
- **Sendler rescue count:** Yad Vashem explicitly states that the exact number saved by Sendler and her partners is unknown. No exact popular total is assigned to one person.
- **Bologna date:** The university describes 1088 as a retrospective convention and its own gradual formation. The entry preserves that qualification and does not claim an exact founding ceremony.
- **Holy Roman origins:** The German Historical Museum’s 962 convention is used for this political band; the Habsburg glossary’s Carolingian antecedent in 800 is explained rather than treated as a conflicting start to be silently discarded.
- **Kyivan Rus’ interval:** 988–1240 selects Christianization through Kyiv’s sack; it is not the lifespan of every Rus’ polity, a constant territory or proof of modern claims.
- **Composite monarchies:** Crown ownership, elective imperial office and actual administration remain distinct. Three Habsburg-specific companion sources have one institutional provenance, so the record does not pretend to possess three independent scholarly witnesses.
- **Fission attribution:** Cambridge’s short archival description compresses the discovery. The narrative distinguishes Meitner/Frisch’s physical interpretation from Hahn/Strassmann’s chemical experiments using the Max Planck account and original-paper metadata.

## Remaining limitations

This batch does not resolve all Nordic, Baltic, Slavic, Balkan, Italian, Iberian or minority histories. Several records have one institutional research base plus companion/primary material rather than two independent scholarly overviews; these limits are visible above. Indexed-only sources warrant future full-text checking. The Chinese text is an original companion synthesis, not a separately peer-reviewed Chinese historiographical survey. Coordinates are representative; event focus intervals are not claims of continuous person presence, settlement lifespan or borders. Biography milestones name institutional reference points where personal attendance is not established.
