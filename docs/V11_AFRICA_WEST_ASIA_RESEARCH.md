# V11 Africa and West/Central Asia research ledger

Checked **2026-10-24** in a browser. Data: `dist/data/expansion-africa-west-asia-v11.json`.

## Contribution and editorial purpose

Eight new bilingual records form four connected pairs: **four biographies and four institutional/social histories**, with **16 dated biography milestones**. Six records belong to Africa and two to West/Central Asia. The pairs deepen the existing `AF2`, `AF4` and `WA2` reading paths rather than introducing a new regional framework. Two biographies concern women. None adds political-overview bars, routes or territorial geometry.

- Ahmad Baba → legal and practical learning in Timbuktu manuscripts → existing Timbuktu and Songhai records.
- Flora Nwapa → book production through Tana Press → Nigerian independence, war and Bessie Head's literary career.
- Doria Shafik → Bint al-Nil's literacy and political work → Huda Shaarawi and the existing 1919–1924 organizing sequence.
- Mahmudhoja Behbudiy → new-method schools, publishing and amateur theater → Samarkand and the existing Bukhara political sequence.

The biographies contain 228–239 English words each; the context narratives contain 164–177. Chinese preserves the same narrative scope, conflict and qualifications. Counts and length describe the contribution, not specialist acceptance or equal regional depth. No V9/V10 source files or imported records were edited.

## V10 evidence-depth follow-up

V10 records a disagreement over the first-publication year of Bessie Head's *When Rain Clouds Gather*: 1968 in Botswana Writers versus 1969 in SAHO. An additional catalogue was inspected:

- [Internet Archive record: When rain clouds gather](https://archive.org/details/whenraincloudsga0000head), checked 2026-10-24, **catalogue metadata only**. The displayed note says the work was originally published in New York by Simon & Schuster in **1968**, and London by Gollancz in **1969**. The scanned item described by this record is a later 1987 edition. No borrowing, full-book reading or copyright-page inspection is claimed.

**Disposition:** This independently accessible catalogue supports retaining the V10 biography's 1968 publication year and shows that a 1969 London edition also existed. It does not prove why SAHO chose 1969 or certify the rest of Head's biography. V10 data remains frozen; this research ledger records the narrower corroboration and leaves source-to-claim review of other inherited assertions open.

## Access limits and omissions

- The Gerda Henkel L.I.S.A. `meet_ahmed_baba` URL redirected to the portal homepage. It is not counted as inspected biography evidence. The separately accessible DW written profile was inspected in its own right.
- The Britannica Ahmad Baba page was blocked by the browser's security policy. It was not accessed through another tool or included as directly inspected evidence.
- Leiden's Flora Nwapa library-weekly page returned a connection reset. Its search snippet is not included as supporting evidence. SAHO and EBSCO were used instead, with their limitations retained.
- B1 supplies most detail about Behbudiy's educational and publishing career. B2 corroborates bounded public roles and premiere chronology, rather than independently corroborating every sentence in B1.
- D1/D2 partly share AUC/family-derived material. T1/T2 are one museum/library interpretive family. More URLs do not erase source dependence.
- Further regional and bilingual specialist review remains desirable, particularly manuscript interpretation, Nwapa's publishing-business chronology, Jadid theater sources and the class reach of Bint al-Nil. These records add depth without claiming to settle those research programs.
