# V10 Americas and Oceania: research and editorial record

Checked: **23 October 2026**. Dataset: `dist/data/expansion-americas-oceania-v10.json`.

## What this addition contains

**30 new entries**: 10 North America, 10 Latin America/Caribbean and 10 Oceania. The set includes **10 biographies with 39 dated milestones**, 10 events, five cultural histories, three ideas/institutions and two places. All entries contain matching original English/Chinese narratives, three concise key facts, explicit date/location notes and at least two existing/new related IDs. English narratives range from 175 to 258 words; biographies meet the 220-word minimum used for this pass. There are 49 unique cited URLs.

The additions develop connected sequences: Indigenous farming and visual culture; multilingual print and migration; agricultural and industrial labour; Caribbean diaspora writing; village archaeology; education and public art; and Pacific land, language and community institutions. They retain V9 content and geometry unchanged. They do not add political timeline rows or territory shapes for people, events or cultures.

## Evidence method and practical limits

- Relevant page bodies were opened and inspected through the research tool, including targeted sections of longer institutional pages. The Smithsonian Garifuna booklet was inspected as extracted PDF text. The Sālote coronation source was inspected catalogue text; neither a complete newspaper nor the image itself is claimed as analyzed.
- Source-level `evidenceNote` fields state the claims and dated milestones supported. Retrieval labels describe access, not a guarantee that every interpretation is settled. A catalogue record can establish an object/date context without supporting a full life story.
- Original bilingual synthesis contains no long quotations. Narratives separate documentary facts from interpretation and avoid absolute first-ever claims when the inspected account is too broad.
- A second URL is not automatically independent evidence. NPS biography/site histories share a source family; Te Ara and NZHistory share Manatū Taonga; the two Cherokee Phoenix pages belong to one university project. Government, museum and archival narratives can also share inherited scholarship.
- Single-family entries are deliberately limited in scope: Hohokam, Cherokee Phoenix, Komagata Maru, Japanese Canadian dispossession, the cannery, Joya de Cerén, Garifuna recordings, Cuba literacy, Tlatelolco, Samoa influenza, Uluṟu handback and the Waitangi Tribunal should receive further community, archival or specialist scholarship before claims are broadened.
- Geographic coordinates are rounded representative editorial points. Sources establish named places and their meaning; they do not validate survey-precision coordinates. A point may mark a homeland, publication city, institutional destination or event venue. Milestones do not manufacture an uninterrupted travel route. The `approximate` flag is reserved for approximate years, not rounded geography.
- Intervals are explicitly identified as lifespans, historical episodes, archaeological phases or documentary/project windows. Huerta’s 1970 endpoint is a selected early-career window. Garifuna 1981 marks field recordings; Taquile 2009 marks a project end. Neither denotes cultural extinction.

## Remaining work before a claim of global parity

This is a connected addition, not a claim that the rest of the world now matches China in depth. Further work should prioritize northern Indigenous community histories, Caribbean languages and women’s organizing, Brazil and the Andes beyond the selected writers/textiles, and Pacific local scholarship. New Guinea was not given a superficial new row to satisfy a geographic quota: V9 already contains Kuk, Massim exchange and wartime Papuan labour, while a new distinct New Guinea biography or community sequence needs locally grounded sources. Banaba’s Rabi community governance, compensation litigation and land claims also deserve a separate researched continuation.

An institutional source can be reliable for dates yet incomplete in whose voices it includes. The next pass should broaden source families, especially where this file uses one accessible institution, and inspect original documents or community testimony before expanding specific disputed claims.
