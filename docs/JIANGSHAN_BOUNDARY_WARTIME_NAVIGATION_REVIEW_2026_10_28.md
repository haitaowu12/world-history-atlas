# Jiangshan boundaries, wartime context and timeline navigation — 28 October 2026

## Geometry and rights review

The original Jiangshan revision reviewed is [`9799f0f61cff80922970d2a0b304e18527db7e1e`](https://github.com/DeanChensj/jiangshan-map/tree/9799f0f61cff80922970d2a0b304e18527db7e1e). Static comparisons of the packed snapshot data, without running upstream code, identified changes to 31 Five Dynasties/Song stage records in [`aa7161e`](https://github.com/DeanChensj/jiangshan-map/commit/aa7161e98e64d6ce2a3857cf1638d5c9aa740ece) and 24 Eastern Jin/Southern and Northern Dynasties stage records in [`957511c`](https://github.com/DeanChensj/jiangshan-map/commit/957511c73ed91718e08ee3d2f2c41b4afa3870fa). These counts are changed stage records, not independent historical validations or a count of unique polygons. Neighbour templates are not assumed to be independent political boundaries.

The [upstream README](https://github.com/DeanChensj/jiangshan-map/blob/9799f0f61cff80922970d2a0b304e18527db7e1e/README.md#credits--license) identifies GPL-3.0 for the project, with separate original terms for prefectural and Warring States data derived from CHGIS/Hartwell. The [CHGIS V6 provider](https://chgis.fas.harvard.edu/data/chgis/v6/) states academic-research use and forbids commercial use, resale **and redistribution**. The source does not identify the derivation and permissions of each changed political outline. A broad assertion about a Khitan or Jin frontier does not verify the individual vertices or the dates of continuous control. No new Jiangshan boundary was imported in this update.

This also affects an **existing provenance question**. The atlas's published V7 phase extraction includes Warring States and administrative schematics from an earlier Jiangshan revision. Comparing corresponding role/title/phase geometries found seven exact matches among 116 comparable Warring States feature instances (all seven reuse one Zhou outline) between that earlier revision and the reviewed upstream revision; this does not identify which, if any, are CHGIS derivatives. The previously stated absence of a direct CHGIS download does not establish that every distributed derivative is free of CHGIS material. The earlier V7 source files remain intact, but their per-feature lineage and permission require an audit and, where needed, clarification from the rights holders or replacement with demonstrably reusable geography. The new review must not be read as a determination that the existing material is cleared for redistribution.

## Independently checked useful content

- The 926 Bohai event keeps its inherited ID and source wording in the review record. Its revised narrative distinguishes the kingdom's defeat and Dongdan administration from the continuity of communities. [Pamela Kyle Crossley's scholarly study](https://ijkh.khistory.org/journal/view.php?number=10) compares written, archaeological and epigraphic evidence; [Asia Society](https://asiasociety.org/education/chinas-liao-dynasty) provides a broad conquest chronology. Neither source validates a drawn border.
- Shen Kuo's 1075 Song–Liao mission is added to his existing biography without changing the released biography input. The [History of Song, volume 331](https://zh.wikisource.org/wiki/宋史/卷331) is a later court account describing the use of archives, meetings and the map compiled on return; [St Andrews](https://mathshistory.st-andrews.ac.uk/Biographies/Shen_Kua/) gives the year. The Kaifeng pin represents preparation at the Song court; the actual meeting site and a full route are not asserted.

## Wartime changes and limits

A 1918 Belgian transition marker keeps the end-of-war context discoverable without drawing a front. The [1914–1918 International Encyclopedia](https://encyclopedia.1914-1918-online.net/article/belgium/) describes the allied offensive from 28 September to the Armistice.

Two **country-reference** hatches were added: Poland in 1941–43, as a broad reference after the German invasion of Soviet-held eastern Poland, and metropolitan France at year-end in 1942–43, including different German and Italian occupation arrangements. The [US Holocaust Memorial Museum's expansion account](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/german-wartime-expansion) and [France account](https://encyclopedia.ushmm.org/content/en/article/france) support the general sequence, not the reference geometry. The shapes are the already attributed CShapes year-end country references, not newly drawn occupation borders. The Poland reference may include small areas with different control, and neither hatch establishes a uniform administration. The France reference excludes overseas territories. In-year changes in 1944 remain place markers, not a reconstructed frontline.

The year buttons and regional focus controls make the selective layer easier to inspect. Focusing temporarily hides modern regional divisions; the user can restore them without changing their saved preference. The existing boundary and marker limitations remain visible in the map key and in each item's evidence.

A [Stanford-derived 1942 ArcGIS teaching map](https://www.arcgis.com/home/item.html?id=81a6388b9a5a437b83bc5a3546b86934) was examined: its author states that its polygons represent annexations and new entities rather than general military occupation, and its terms restrict reuse to noncommercial scholarly work. It was not imported as an occupation map. The [Rauchway teaching maps](https://github.com/erauchway/ww2-maps/tree/4f24a056cb1a130507d825381d6b5cf3019f14f0) carry an MIT repository notice, but their own documentation cites other geographical sources, including Stanford-derived material; an overall software notice does not settle every polygon's provenance. Their geometry was not imported.

## Timeline navigation

The comparison, period guides and calendars now retain the same three navigation choices. The period guide's map action had collided with the regional browser's `reference-browse` action; it now has a separate action and is checked against the actual dispatch order. The comparison view has a labeled time scrubber, one-year keyboard movement and previous/next-window controls. Filter and slider focus is restored after the view is rebuilt.
