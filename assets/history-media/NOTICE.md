## Required retained attribution

The Kartini image is supplied under **CC BY-SA 3.0**, credited to **Collectie Wereldmuseum (v/h Tropenmuseum), part of the National Museum of World Cultures**. The 534 × 700 image is distributed unchanged. Reusers must retain attribution, link the license and identify their changes; adaptations must meet the share-alike terms.

The Liliʻuokalani image credits photographer **James J. Williams**, the **Library of Congress**, and digital restorer **Adam Cuerden**. The source file page records the public-domain status of the photograph and the restorer’s permission.

The Met images are the museum’s provided web-size files. The item pages mark the works Public Domain and the museum’s [Open Access policy](https://www.metmuseum.org/policies/image-resources) supplies CC0 image rights.

The National Palace Museum images are public 600-pixel presentation details from its object pages. Those pages specify CC0 for lower/presentation images and CC BY 4.0 for higher-resolution downloads. The atlas includes the presentation files, retaining photographic scales and color targets.

Commons images retain the file-specific public-domain basis or license linked above. The atlas has not applied local crops, recoloring, or generative edits.
