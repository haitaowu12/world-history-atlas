## Scope and attribution

The china-events and china-library datasets adapt the event wording, bilingual dictionary, person/profile records, place notes, historical chronicles, era-name list, and schematic corridor records from js/map_data.js, js/lore_data.js and js/i18n_dict.js at this exact commit. Original Chinese wording and source English translations are inherited except for the explicitly documented neutral display amendments in china-import-manifest.json. Programmatic normalization and the importer are authored for this atlas.

## Historical evidence

The original project's README names Tan Qixiang's Historical Atlas of China, CHGIS (Harvard-Yenching Institute and Fudan University), Robert M. Hartwell datasets, and aourednik/historical-basemaps. These are collection-level acknowledgements, not item-level corroborating citations for every imported narrative. An inherited entry is therefore presented as a reference-project record pending independent claim review. Classical quotations and the source English translations are preserved as licensed upstream material; they have not each been checked against critical editions.
