# Warring States c. 250 BCE — source and reuse

The source illustration is [Warring States c. 250 BC.svg](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Warring_States_c._250_BC.svg), by **Ian Remsen**. The author identifies it as their own work and dedicates it under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Its source description identifies public-domain Natural Earth geography, the Albers projection and political context from *The Cambridge History of China*, vol. 1, p. 39. This notice preserves those distinctions; the source illustration's dedication does not relicense the Cambridge book.

The atlas extracted the seven colored state paths, sampled their curves and registered them to geographic coordinates using the author's stated projection, the marked Luoyang location and Natural Earth coastline checks. The derived geometry is released under CC0 1.0 to the extent of the atlas's rights. The original illustration and its checksum are retained in `references/china-open-boundaries/`; the transformations and limits are documented in the boundary audit.

This map is an approximate interpretation around 250 BCE. The atlas displays it as a fixed dated reference for 250–231 BCE, not as a reconstruction of annual changes. Seven separate colors represent named states; an empty area is not evidence of an uninhabited or ungoverned area.
